Our Mission

CommUnityChest is built on the belief that communities thrive when we share resources, skills, and care for one another outside of the traditional market.

By facilitating direct person-to-person connections, we create a space where resources can be shared, needs can be met, and community bonds can be strengthened.

When contacting each other and exhcanging, please be safe and respectful.
